## 2. Screen Breakdown & Taste Decisions

### Slide 1: Desktop Split View (Intake + EMR)
- **Patient Side (Left)**: Warm cream canvas, uncluttered typography, large tactile option cards, progress track, and voice assist button.
- **Doctor Side (Right)**: Dark emerald console displaying real-time genetic risk gauge, automated SOAP clinical note, and live JSON schema.

### Slide 2: Mobile Tap-First Question Flow
- **55-Year-Old Friendly**: 52px touch targets, zero confusing medical jargon, expandable cards for products & procedures (avoiding broken 20-cell mobile tables).

### Slide 3: Visual Hair Loss Pattern Selector (Q4)
- **Visual Norwood & Ludwig Scales**: Clear illustrated scalp cards for Receding Hairline, Thinning at Crown, Widening Part, Diffuse Loss, Patchy Loss, and Sudden Shedding. Active card glows with radiant emerald border.

### Slide 4: "Story Mode" / Voice Express Pass
- **Multimodal Hinglish Intake**: Audio sound wave visualizer, real-time speech-to-text transcript, and glowing green checkmark badges populating 10+ questions simultaneously in < 30 seconds.

### Slide 5: Doctor EMR Dashboard & Schema Inspector
- **Clinic Handoff**: Triage gauges (Genetic Risk, TE Index), automated SOAP note (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan), validated JSON output tree matching `intake-schema.json`, and 1-click Download / Print buttons.
